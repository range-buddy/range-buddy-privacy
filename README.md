# Privacy Policy for RangeBuddy

**Last updated: April 16, 2026**

RangeBuddy ("we," "us," or "our") operates the RangeBuddy mobile application (the "App"). This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

---

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Videos and photos** you record or upload within the app
- **Text content** including swing notes, tags, session titles, and diary entries
- **Ratings** you assign to your swings

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Anonymous user identifier** — We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to identify your account without requiring an email address or password. This ID is randomly generated and not linked to your real identity unless you choose to sign in with Google.
- **Usage data** — Basic crash reports and performance data collected via Firebase to help us improve the app.

### If You Sign In with Google
- Your Google account name and email address are stored with your account so you can recover your data across devices.

---

## How We Use Your Information

- To store and sync your swing videos, notes, and sessions across your devices
- To provide the core features of the app (library, diary, compare view)
- To diagnose crashes and improve app performance

We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** use your data for advertising.

---

## Camera, Microphone, and Photo Library

RangeBuddy requests access to:
- **Camera** — to record golf swing videos directly in the app
- **Microphone** — to record audio alongside swing videos
- **Photo Library** — to select existing videos from your device

This access is used only to provide core app functionality. Media is stored in your Firebase account and is not shared with any third party.

---

## Data Storage

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Storage), operated by Google LLC. Data is stored in the United States. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details on how Google handles data.

---

## Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data by contacting us (see below). Anonymous accounts that have been inactive for more than 180 days may be automatically deleted.

---

## Children's Privacy

RangeBuddy is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

---

## Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, additional rights may apply under GDPR or CCPA respectively.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Hosting This Policy

Both the Apple App Store and Google Play require a publicly accessible URL for your privacy policy. Options:
- **GitHub Pages**: Create a public repo, add this as `index.md`, enable Pages → your URL will be `https://yourusername.github.io/rangebuddy-privacy`
- **Notion**: Paste this into a Notion page and set it to public
- **Firebase Hosting**: Deploy this as a static page alongside the web app
